The core insight is that start_new_session makes the server PID a stable PGID that remains valid after the leader exits, so getpgid() and the poll() guard were unnecessary and actively harmful. Test finalizer now checks returncode before its emergency killpg to avoid PID/PGID reuse, and closing the worker socket releases the orphaned child on failure paths.
